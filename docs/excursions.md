# Excursions

During a longer trip you often head out for a day or two with a smaller bag: a day hike, a night in a mountain hut, a
boat trip. An **excursion** is that bag's own packing list. It lives inside the trip, you tick it on the morning you
leave — however long ago the suitcase was packed — and whatever you put together once can be used again on the next
trip.

## Creating one

Open the trip and tap the **Ausflüge** icon (a signpost) in the row under the trip's name, then the round **+** at the
bottom right. The sheet asks for:

- a **Name** — *Tageswanderung*, *Hüttentour*;
- **Von** and **Bis** — the day or days. Both are optional: leave them empty while the weather decides;
- **Wer geht mit** — **Alle**, or tap the people who go;
- **Beginnen mit** — a group to start from (search it by its name or by something in it), or **Leer beginnen**.

Tap **Ausflug anlegen**. The excursion's list opens, filled from the group. A message at the bottom offers
**Rückgängig** if you picked the wrong group.

### What it does to the packing list

While the trip has not started, anything the excursion needs from home **is added to the trip's packing list too**, so
it does not stay at home — the sheet says so before you tap. Something the packing list already has is not added a
second time: if the list has one water bottle and the hike needs one, the hike borrows it. If the hike needs more than
the list has, the list's amount goes up. Something you marked **bewusst nicht mitgenommen** on the packing list is
left that way.

On the packing list, a row an excursion borrows names that excursion in a small line under the row's name, so you do
not leave it out of the suitcase by accident.

Once the trip is under way — started, its first day come, or the packing finished — the packing list is left alone.
Things the luggage does not have show **nicht im Gepäck** on the excursion, with **Vor Ort besorgen** beside them.

## Packing the bag

The excursion's list works like the packing list, only smaller:

- tick a line when it is in the bag — this tick belongs to the excursion; the suitcase's tick is not touched;
- **aus dem Gepäck** under a line means it comes out of your luggage;
- a pin glyph on a line means you buy it on the way — lunch at the kiosk. These lines also appear on the trip's shopping list
  (**Einkaufen**), under **Vor Ort** and the excursion's name. Tick one off there and the excursion says **vor Ort
  gekauft**; it still needs to go into the bag;
- a thing you bought on the spot and want to keep — a rain cape from the hut — gets **Auf die Packliste** beside
  **vor Ort gekauft**. Tap it and the thing joins the trip's packing list, already packed, and your inventory, so the
  next trip can plan with it. **Rückgängig** in the message takes it back;
- tap a line for more: **Eins mehr**, **Eins weniger**, **Gekauft**, **Vor Ort besorgen**, **Diesmal nicht**,
  **Von der Liste nehmen**.

### A thing for each person

The excursion's list works like the packing list: add with the orange **＋** — search your inventory, pick a whole
group, or create a new item. Some things each person needs — a sleeping bag, a water bottle. Above the field, choose
who a new thing is for before you add it: **Gemeinsam** (one for everyone), **Alle** (one each) or single people. A
thing for each person shows once with its people; tap it to see one line per person, each ticked on its own.

If you change who goes later (⋮ → **Ausflug bearbeiten**), things you added for **Alle** follow: someone who joins gets
their own line, someone who stays behind loses the lines not yet packed. A line of theirs already in the bag stays,
marked **nicht mehr dabei**, with **Herausnehmen** to take it off the list.

## Using it again

In the excursion's ⋮, **Als Gruppe speichern** turns its list into a group. Start your next excursion — on this trip or
another — from that group, or add it to a trip like any other group. Things for each person stay *for each person*,
things bought on the spot stay *vor Ort*.

**Ausflug löschen** in the same menu removes the excursion and its list. The trip's packing list stays as it is.

## Reminders

On the day before an excursion and on the day itself, while something on its list is still open, the dashboard shows
it (*„Morgen: Hüttentour"*). With a server, the morning reminder also sends a notification to the people who go — see
[Notifications & Push](notifications.md); switch it off under **Ausflüge** in Settings.

## Modes and backup

Excursions work in all three modes. **Local Mode** has no server and therefore no reminder notification — the
dashboard is the reminder. Excursions are **not in the portable backup** file, like a trip's tasks and notes; a group
you saved them as is.
