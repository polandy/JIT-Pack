# Meal plan

In a holiday flat somebody cooks every evening, and somebody has to buy what goes into it. **Essen** is the trip's
meal plan: what you eat on each day, who cooks it, and what is still to buy for it. The ingredients you write into a
meal are on the shopping list straight away, and every meal stands on the [day plan](day-plan.md).

## Opening it

The meal plan is the **knife and fork** — the last icon in the row under the trip's name, after the day plan. Like the
day plan, it is there only once the trip has both a start and an end date.

## Reading it

The days stand one under another, from today during the trip (the first day before it). The days already behind fold
into one line on top — tap it to see them. Each day shows its **Frühstück**, **Mittag** and **Abend**: a meal with its
dish, who cooks it, how much is bought (in words and as a ring), or an empty dashed **＋ Abendessen** to plan one. A
snack or an afternoon cake has its own place too — **＋ Zwischendurch** at the end of the day.

While anything is still to buy, a bar on top says how much, and how much of it is for today. **Einkaufen ›** opens the
shopping list.

## Planning a meal

Tap an empty meal, or the round **+** for the next free one. The sheet asks for:

- **Gericht** — the dish. As you type, dishes you cooked on earlier trips are offered with their ingredients
  (*„Rösti mit Spiegelei · 4 Zutaten · Tessin"*). Tap one and its ingredients are filled in; change them as you like.
- **Selbst kochen** or **Auswärts**. A meal eaten out has no ingredients, just **Wo** — your shortlisted
  [ideas](ideas.md) are offered there, so the restaurant you picked is one tap away.
- **Mahlzeit** and **Tag**, to move it; **Uhrzeit** if it has a time (without one it stands where its meal belongs —
  breakfast in the morning, dinner in the evening); a **Notiz**.
- **Wer kocht** — with a server, once more than one person is on the trip.
- **Zutaten** — type *„500 g Hörnli"* and press Enter: the amount is split off by itself, and the field is ready for
  the next one.

**Hinzufügen** writes it. Tap a meal to change it; **Mahlzeit löschen** deletes it with its ingredients — the question
says how many open ones leave the shopping list.

## The shopping list

Every ingredient is a line of **Einkaufen**, under **Essensplan**, in the order of the meals, with its amount and its
meal under it (*„1 kg · Mo. Abend · Raclette"*). Buy it there or tick it in the meal — it is the same tick, and both
places show it at once. An ingredient moves into **Fällig** on the day of its meal; the days before, it waits under
**Essensplan**. Once the meal's day has passed, whatever was not bought leaves the open list — you ate something else.

Before the trip, tap **Vor Ort** beside an ingredient to buy it at home instead (**Vor der Reise**) — the cheese you
bring from your own cheese shop.

## On the day plan and the dashboard

Each meal stands on the [day plan](day-plan.md) at its time, or where its meal belongs, with who cooks and how much is
bought. Tap it to open the same sheet. During the trip the overview shows **Heute essen** under the trip: today's meals
and what is still missing for them.

## A picnic on an excursion

On a day with an [excursion](excursions.md), a breakfast, lunch or snack can go along: switch on **Mit auf den Ausflug**
in the meal. It then stands as one line on the excursion's packing list — tick it when it is in the rucksack, and it
counts in what is packed. Its ingredients stay on the shopping list.

## Modes and backup

The meal plan works in all three modes; only **Wer kocht** needs a server with more than one person on the trip. In
**Local Mode** the earlier dishes come from the trips on this device. Meals are **not in the portable backup** file,
like a trip's tasks, notes and excursions.
