# What you forgot last time

When you plan a trip that resembles an earlier one, the packing list opens with a card
headed **Letztes Mal vergessen**. It names the earlier trip and lists what was missing
there, so the same gap is not left open twice.

## Which earlier trip

The card reads **one** trip and names it, so you always know where its claim comes from:

- the most recent **archived** trip of the **same series** (the series is chosen in the
  trip wizard under *Mehr Optionen*, or later in the trip's *Reise-Eigenschaften* —
  there even on a finished trip, so an older trip can join a series after the fact), or,
  where there is none,
- the most recent archived trip built from the **same Ferien-Vorlage**. A shared group
  alone does not count — a camera group goes to the beach and the mountains alike.

"Most recent" means the latest year, then the latest start date. A trip with neither a
series nor a Ferien-Vorlage gets no card.

## What it lists

Every item that trip recorded as *missing*:

- **Fehlte auf der Liste** — added on the way, so it was bought or borrowed rather than
  packed;
- **Vergessen einzupacken** — on the list, but it stayed at home. This includes a row you judged **Hätte mit
  gemusst** when closing the trip ([Judging a trip afterwards](tasks-and-shopping.md#judging-a-trip-afterwards)).

Under each item stand the comments written on it during that trip, newest first, with
the date and, on a shared server, who wrote it.

## Acting on it

- **Auf die Liste** puts the item on the new trip's list, once for the whole trip, with
  anything it always brings along. The entry then leaves the card.
- An item you add any other way leaves the card too: the card compares itself with the
  list each time, by inventory item or by name.
- **Ausblenden** hides the card for this trip. The choice is kept on this device only;
  another phone will still show it, and the next trip of the series shows it again.

When nothing is left to show, the card is gone. It appears only while a trip is being
planned — once the trip has started, the list's head belongs to packing.

## Single-User and Local Mode

The card works the same way. With no accounts to name, a comment shows its date alone.

On a multi-user server, an earlier trip's list arrives on a device the first time it is
needed. Until then — offline, say — the card names the trip and says its list is not
loaded yet, rather than staying silent, which would read as *nothing was forgotten*.
