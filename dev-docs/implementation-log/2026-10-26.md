## An absence the snackbar grants by itself (2026-10-27)

E2E-M25-24 was written to assert that M25's *„Nothing to change"* snackbar offers no undo, and passed at once — then
passed just as well with the assertion flipped to `toHaveCount(1)`. Both are retrying assertions: the first waits
until no *Undo* button is left, and the snackbar lapses by itself after `TOAST_DURATION_MS`, well inside the expect
timeout. Any absence asserted with a retrying matcher on something that goes away by itself is green on every build.
The case now reads the standing toast's buttons in one `evaluate`, which needs the toast to exist (the positive
signal) and takes a single reading.

Read that way, the snackbar did carry *Undo*: `afterBatch` used `announceAct`, whose snackbar always arms the button,
and the record behind it was still the previous act's — `announce` disarms the outgoing toast's record only through
its dismiss handler, which the ordering deliberately defeats so a second pack keeps its own undo. Tapping *Undo* on
„nothing to change" took back the batch before it. `announceNotice` now says a sentence about no act: same
snackbar, no button, record cleared. Items and shopping were already right — both present a plain toast.

## A filter does not stop a toast lying on top (2026-10-27)

E2E-M28-11 and E2E-FLOW-02 filtered Bob's notice by its own text, on the premise that a parallel case's toast for the
same account was only a strict-mode problem. It is a pointer problem too: every notification toast opens at the top
for 6 s, so a newer one lies exactly over an older one's *Open*. Run 40415032465's first attempt shows it — Bob's
*Open* on „Tiscali" was intercepted by location.spec's „Alice suggested Rundweg" from the other worker. Any `server`
file where Alice adds an idea on a trip shared with Bob notifies him.

The two clicking recipients are now `erin` and `frank`, each logged in by one case, under carol's and dave's rule
(`mockIdp.mjs`). Serialising the files that notify Bob was rejected: it costs the leg its second worker on those files,
and every later file that notifies him would have to remember to join. Queueing notification toasts in the app — the
defect is real for a user too (TODO.md) — does not fix the case on its own: the notice would wait behind a foreign
toast for up to 6 s, past the 5 s expect timeout, which turns the interception into a timing failure.
