# 3.32 The Activity Log — Who Changed What

**Status: accepted** — **implemented** (ADR-084). A trip is worked on by several people on several devices, and
"who packed the Wanderschuhe?", "who deleted the sunscreen?" or "what changed in the inventory this week?" had no
answer anywhere: the conflict log (G-2) records only merges that lost a field. The activity log records every change
the server applied, with the person who made it. The screen is **M30** (UI-Spec).

* **FR-32.1 (The Record):** Every write the server applies — a push on either partition, a conflict revert, an item's
  photo, an idea's picture — writes one entry in `activity_log`, inside the write's own transaction: the table and row,
  whether it created, changed or deleted the row, **the account that pushed it** (the session's, never a claim of the
  envelope — invariant 3), the server's time, and every field the write actually changed as *[before, after]*. A delete
  keeps what the row held, *[before, null]*. The entry stores the row's **name** and the name of **what it belongs to**
  (an excursion line's excursion, a vote's idea, a reply's thread) as they were, so it stays readable after the row is
  gone; each syncable table declares where its names come from. A write that changes nothing — a re-sent value, a merge
  the row's newer clock outranked, a replay, a refusal — records nothing. The entry is filed under **the trip the
  change is about**: a trip's own rows, and its master-partition rows too (its name and dates, its roster), are that
  trip's; everything else is the inventory's. A trip's log goes with the trip, and its deletion is recorded nowhere.
  **The log starts when this is deployed**: nothing before recorded who made a change. Nothing is pruned, like the
  conflict log (a household's log stays small). It is read, never synced.
* **FR-32.2 (Reading It):** The client decides what an entry means (invariant 4, `domain/activity.ts`, and a feature
  module's own reader for its tables): added, deleted, changed; packed, unpacked, *bewusst nicht eingepackt*; bought and
  taken back; a task done or reopened; a note ticked or un-ticked; a vote cast or withdrawn; reordered; hidden and shown
  again (FR-24.3). A G-3 claim alone and the generation's bookkeeping (`trip_generated_positions`,
  `trip_template_sources`, `trip_applied_changes`) are no act and are not shown. Each line names the thing, the act, the
  part of the app (*Packliste, Einkauf, Aufgaben, Notizen, Ausflüge, Ideen, Mitglieder, Reise, Inventar, …*), the person
  and the time; a *changed* line names the fields a reader can read, before → after. **Consecutive lines by one person
  of one act in one place on one day fold into one** — *„Badehose, Socken, Zahnbürste · 3× eingepackt · Bob · 21:24"* —
  which opens to its parts; a run somebody else interrupted is two lines. Days are headed *Heute, Gestern*, then the
  date. Newest first, a page at a time.
* **FR-32.3 (Two Logs):** A trip's log is read by its members (`GET /trips/{id}/activity`), from M4's ⋮. The
  inventory's (`GET /master/activity`) is read by every account from M9's ⋮, filtered like a master pull: shared master
  data is everyone's, a series only its owner's.
* **Modes.** **Server:** as above. **Single-User:** recorded and shown, without names — there is one person.
  **Local:** there is no server and so no record; both entries are hidden (G-8). **Not in the portable backup.**
