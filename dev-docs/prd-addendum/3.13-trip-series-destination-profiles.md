# 3.13 Trip Series & Destination Profiles

* **FR-13.1 (Trip Series):** Trips can be assigned to a named *Trip Series* (e.g., "Samedan Summer", "Samedan Winter").
  A series groups recurring trips to the same destination or of the same type and serves as the anchor for historical
  comparisons (3.14), attribute defaults (FR-15.1), and cloning defaults (FR-12.1). **A series name is unique
  instance-wide** (`trip_series` UNIQUE (name)): a series is the anchor a whole
  household's history hangs on, and two "Samedan Sommer" owned by two accounts would split that history in two while
  looking like one thing on every screen. Same reasoning, and the same accepted offline cost, as FR-1.6's template
  names. **The wizard answers it where the name is typed:** M3's *neue Serie* field checks the typed
  name against the series the device already holds and, when it is taken, names the existing series and blocks the step
  instead of writing a second one. It deliberately does **not** attach the trip to the existing series by itself — the
  select right above the field already offers it, and quietly deciding whose series a trip joins is not the wizard's
  call. M16's rename is refused the same way and the field goes back to the stored name.
* **FR-13.2 (Destination Profiles):** A series can carry a *Destination Profile* with persistent, destination-bound
  data: notes (e.g., "washing machine available"), local conditions, and reusable destination checklists.
* **FR-13.3 (Destination-Bound Shopping Lists):** Recurring BUY_LOCAL lists (e.g., a grocery list for a specific holiday
  location) can be stored on the Destination Profile and are automatically offered when a new trip in that series is
  generated.
* **FR-13.4 (A series carries tasks for every trip in it, *built*, ADR-106):** some of what a recurring trip needs
  done comes back every time and belongs to the place, not to the luggage — *„Steinbock-Wanderung reservieren"* four
  weeks before Samedan, every summer. A template's trip tasks (FR-7.4) do not fit: the Ferien-Vorlage serves every
  destination. The **destination profile** (FR-13.2) therefore carries **series tasks**, which become open trip todos
  on every trip of the series.
  * **Storage:** a master table `destination_tasks` (`profile_id`, `task`, `lead_days`, `assignee_user_id`, field
    HLCs) — `template_tasks`' shape and reason (two devices, two tasks, no overwrite). Writing the first one creates
    the profile lazily, as a note or a checklist entry does.
  * **When it is due: a lead before the trip's start, or none.** `lead_days` is NULL for no due day, otherwise one of
    *Am Vortag*, 3 days, 1, 2, 4 or 8 weeks — FR-29.21's four leads and two longer ones, because a series books
    further ahead than an excursion. **No fixed yearly date:** a calendar date breaks the moment the trip moves by a
    week, and a date before the trip is generated would simply be missed. *Revisit trigger:* a booking that opens on a
    fixed date regardless of the trip.
  * **The due day follows the trip's start** (ADR-106, ADR-108). The todo stores `anchor_kind` `trip_start` and its
    lead in `anchor_lead_days`, never a day: its due day is the trip's `start_date` less the lead, derived by the
    same task projection and reminder query that derive an idea's preparation step (FR-29.21, ADR-104). Moving the
    trip moves the todo. A trip with no start date (FR-2.1a) gives it no due day until it has one. A lead already
    past reads *Überfällig*, as ADR-104 settled — nothing is clamped to today. Choosing a fixed day on the todo's
    sheet (FR-7.11) drops the lead.
  * **Who does it: an optional default assignee.** A series task may name a user; the todo is assigned to them when
    they are a member of the trip, otherwise it starts unassigned (FR-7.5). This is the case FR-7.5's revisit trigger
    named — the same task given to the same person by hand on every trip — and it arises on a series, not on a
    template that serves every destination. The seat offers the instance's users, since a series has no roster.
    **G-8:** Local and Single-User Mode show no seat and store none.
  * **Where it materialises.** Every trip **generated into the series** gets them (M3) — deduplicated against the
    template's own trip tasks by exact trimmed text, FR-7.4's rule, and counted on step 3's summary on a line of their
    own. **Copying the series' last trip** (FR-12.1) is a new trip in the series and adds them the same way. A trip
    that **joins the series later** — on M22 (FR-2.7) or by M16's *attach* — is **offered** them in a sheet: each
    task ticked, a task the trip already holds (same trimmed text) named and left out; *„Nicht jetzt"* keeps the
    series and adds nothing. **Not offered on an archived trip**, which joins a series only to file its record, and
    not when nothing is left to offer. Detaching a trip keeps its todos.
  * **The todo remembers where it came from:** `comments.destination_task_id` (`ON DELETE SET NULL`, `idea_id`'s
    shape) carries the origin line *„🔁 Samedan Sommer"* on M25, named after the trip's series. It is ticked,
    reassigned, retagged and deleted like any trip todo.
  * **Deliberate non-features** (FR-7.4's, for the same reasons): a series task edited or added on M16 is **not
    pushed** into trips already generated — it reaches the next one (*trigger:* a series task added and expected on
    a running trip); trip todos do not flow back into the series (*trigger:* the same todo typed by hand on a second
    trip of the series); not in the portable format or Local Mode's backup, like the rest of the profile.
  * **Three modes:** the tasks, their materialisation and the offer are client-side (invariant 4) and work alike in
    all three; only the assignee seat is server-only (G-8), and the `task_due` reminder needs a server (FR-7.11).
