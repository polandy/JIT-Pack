# 3.33 The Meal Plan — What the Family Eats, Day by Day

**Status: accepted** — **implemented** (ADR-092). A holiday flat means cooking, and cooking means a list: what is eaten
on which day, who cooks it, and what still has to be bought for it. The **meal plan** is that list. It lives inside a
trip, puts each meal on the day plan (§3.29), and its ingredients **are** lines of the shopping list (§3.30) rather than
entries copied there. The concept and its rendered variants were settled on 2026-10-04 in the mockup
`mockup-meal-plan.html` (owner's choices: a view of its own, one heading on the shopping list, the days one under
another; a snack slot, the picnic in the rucksack and the dashboard block in the first version). The screen is **M31**
(UI-Spec).

* **FR-33.1 (The Meal):** A trip carries any number of **meals**: a **day** of the trip (a calendar date), a **slot** —
  *Frühstück*, *Mittagessen*, *Zwischendurch*, *Abendessen* (`breakfast`, `lunch`, `snack`, `dinner`) — a **dish**
  (its title), a **kind** — *selbst kochen* (`cook`) or *auswärts* (`out`) — and optionally a time, a note, **who
  cooks** (a member, FR-33.8) and, eaten out, **where** (free text). They live in the table `meals` in the **trip
  partition** and sync like every other trip row (field-level LWW, tombstones, they go with their trip). A slot is a
  place on the day, not a uniqueness rule: two devices planning the same dinner offline both keep theirs, and the day
  shows both. A meal without a time stands at its slot's place — breakfast 08:00, lunch 12:30, snack 15:30, dinner
  18:30 — which orders it and nothing else; the time it shows is the slot's word.
* **FR-33.2 (Its Ingredients):** a cooked meal carries **ingredients**: a name, an **amount** as free text
  (*„500 g"*, *„1 Glas"* — never summed or converted), the list it is bought on (`buy_before` or `buy_local`,
  FR-30.1's vocabulary) and its order in the meal. They live in `meal_ingredients`, in the trip partition, and go with
  their meal. Writing *„500 g Hörnli"* into the ingredient field splits a leading amount off the name. A meal eaten out
  has none; turning a cooked meal into one eaten out takes its ingredients away when it is saved.
* **FR-33.3 (The Ingredients Are Lines of the Shopping List):** each ingredient of a meal is a line of M6 — a
  projection through `lib/shoppingSources.ts`, like the packing list's buy rows (FR-30.2) and an excursion's *vor Ort*
  lines (FR-31.8), **never a copy in `shopping_entries`**. Buying it on M6 or ticking it in the meal is one write: the
  ingredient's `bought_at` and `bought_by_user_id`, FR-30.4's stamp, the buyer stamped by the server (invariant 3).
  * **One heading.** The open ingredients of every meal stand under one heading of their own, *🍽 Essensplan*, after
    the combined packing heading and the excursions', in the order of their meals (day, then slot), each naming its
    meal on its second line — *„1 kg · Mo. Abend · Raclette"*, after its amount — and in the *Fällig* block the
    heading's name where an entry names its tag. A heading per meal was rejected: a week of
    dinners is fourteen headings. Two ingredients of the same name (*Kartoffeln* for two meals) stay two lines, as an
    entry and a packing row do (FR-30.2). An ingredient is never a drop target or a place to file an entry, and is
    placed by FR-30.13 inside its heading like any line.
  * **Due on its meal's day.** An ingredient on *Vor Ort* is due on its meal's day, one on *Vor der Reise* on the eve
    of departure (FR-30.10's *Vor Abreise*). **It enters the *Fällig* block on its due day only**, not two days ahead
    as an entry does: a week of meals would otherwise fill the block with the next three days' cooking. Before that
    day it stands under *Essensplan* with its due pill.
  * **After the meal.** Once its meal's day has passed, an ingredient still open **leaves the open list**: eaten is
    eaten. A bought one stays under its list's *gekauft* fold as long as its meal exists.
  * **Before the trip's first day** an ingredient may be put on *Vor der Reise* (*„Raclettekäse von zuhause"*);
    from that day on every new ingredient is on *Vor Ort* and the choice is not offered.
  * The morning reminder (FR-30.10, FR-7.11) does not read ingredients: the meal plan is looked at on the day, and the
    dashboard block (FR-33.7) says what is open.
* **FR-33.4 (Earlier Dishes):** a new meal's dish field offers the dishes of **earlier meals** — the meals of every
  other trip on the device, newest first, one per title — with their ingredients and where they came from (*„4
  Zutaten · Tessin, Mai 2026"*). Taking one fills the dish and the ingredients (names, amounts, all on *Vor Ort*,
  none bought), which stay editable. There is **no recipe book**: what the family cooks is in its trips, as what it
  packs is in its packing lists. The rule is client-side (invariant 4), so Local Mode has it.
* **FR-33.5 (On the Day Plan):** each meal is a line of M29's timeline on its day — a projection through
  `lib/dayPlanSources.ts`, kind *meal* — at its time or its slot's place: the slot's name as its label, the dish, who
  cooks, how many of its ingredients are bought as a ring and in words (*„2 von 4 Zutaten eingekauft"*), or
  *auswärts* and where. A tap opens the meal's sheet over the day plan. A day's strip tile counts meals like any line.
* **FR-33.6 (The Picnic in the Rucksack):** a cooked meal on a day an excursion covers — any slot but dinner — may be
  **taken on that excursion** (`meals.excursion_id`). It then stands as **one line** on the excursion's list (M27),
  *„🍽 Picknick am Gletscher"*, packed and unpacked there with its own state on the meal (`excursion_packed_at`), and
  counted in the excursion's packed share; its ingredients stay on the shopping list under *Essensplan*. The line is
  not a copy in `excursion_items`, and it takes no part in FR-31.4's borrowing. The day plan's excursion line names
  it. Moving the meal to a day the excursion does not cover, or making it a meal eaten out, takes it off the
  excursion; deleting the excursion leaves the meal.
* **FR-33.7 (Today's Meals on the Dashboard):** on a trip's days M1 carries ***Heute essen*** under the trip, like
  FR-29.7's *Heute* card and through the same seam (`lib/tripCards.ts`), and inside the hero as a block once the
  packing is finished (FR-7.10): today's meals in their order, each with its slot, its dish, who cooks and *„N Zutaten
  offen"* or *alles da*; a tap opens the meal's sheet over the dashboard, *Essensplan ›* the view. Absent before and
  after the trip and on a day without meals. The *Heute* card leaves the meals to it: one place for each.
* **FR-33.8 (Who Cooks):** a cooked meal may name **one member** of the trip who cooks it (`cook_user_id`, the
  client's to set like FR-30.12's assignee — invariant 3 concerns who acted). Offered in Server Mode while the trip
  has more than one member; Single-User and Local Mode name nobody, as M28 does. Nobody is notified.
* **FR-33.9 (Deleting a Meal):** deleting a meal takes **its ingredients with it**, bought or not, and its line on an
  excursion; the confirmation says how many open ingredients leave the shopping list. A bought ingredient is a
  purchase of that meal, and kept without its meal it would be a line naming nothing.
* **FR-33.10 (Module Boundary):** the meal plan's client code is a feature module of its own, `client/src/meals/`
  (store, actions, M31, the sheet, its pure rules in `meals/domain/`, its words in `meals/i18n/`), its Playwright cases
  under `client/e2e/meals/`. It meets the shopping list, the day plan, the excursions and the dashboard only through
  kernel contracts that `App.vue` binds, and imports none of them; `scripts/module-boundary-gate.mjs` holds it.
* **FR-33.11 (Modes):** all three. Single-User and Local Mode have everything but *who cooks*; Local Mode keeps the
  rows on the device, and its suggestions come from the trips on it. The portable backup does not carry meals (the
  standing decision on trip content beside the packing list, `backlog.md`).
